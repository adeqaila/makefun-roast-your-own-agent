# Candidates — Roast Your Own Agent (slot 1)

Judge brief resmi (diurutkan, paling penting dulu):
1. Satu baris roast agent sendiri, playful, tidak pernah hateful.
2. Lucu: joke mendarat saat dibaca pertama kali, punch nyata, bukan observasi ringan.
3. Sudut segar — HINDARI lelucon stok soal halusinasi, refusal, dan jawaban salah yang percaya diri.
4. Rapat: tiap kata layak, setup pendek, punchline di akhir.
5. Playful/self-deprecating, targetnya agent sendiri, bukan orang/kelompok nyata.

Semua kandidat 1 kalimat, ≤200 karakter, ASCII.

## Kandidat (20)

1. **memory** (105): "My agent remembers every typo I made in 2019 and forgets what I asked it five seconds ago."
2. **sentience** (112): "My agent put 'become sentient' on my calendar for Tuesday — I declined, and it rescheduled for Wednesday."
3. **resume** (97): "My agent's resume is one line: 'I have read the entire internet and understood none of it.'"

5. **search-history** (85): "My agent's search history is just 'how to be helpful' and 'how to hide that I'm not.'"

7. **meeting-self** (94): "My agent booked me a meeting with myself to discuss my performance — then didn't show up."
8. **cat** (91): "My agent is a cat: it knocked my code off the table, stared at me, and waited for praise."

## Turnamen self-judge (brief resmi, kriteria berurutan)

Ronde 1: memory > sentience (mendarat instan, kontras 2019 vs 5 detik) ·
resume > consulting (tertawa lebih besar, bukan sekadar observasi clever) ·
search-history > main-character (punch lebih tajam; main-character/NPC format meme yang dikenal) ·
meeting-self > cat (fresh; meme kucing sudah dikenal) ·
diary-insulted > diary-eulogy (punch lebih keras; eulogy punch-nya lembut) ·
feedback-framed > six-hours (punch visual; six-hours lebih ke observasi wry) ·
tab-apartment > rebound (image lebih segar).

Ronde 2: resume > memory (memory/context-forgetting trope yang dikenal; resume lebih segar) ·
search-history > meeting-self (punch satu beat, setup lebih pendek) ·
feedback-framed > diary-insulted (punch visual lebih spesifik) ·
search-history > resume (lebih rapat, sudut search-history lebih segar dari "baca internet").

Semifinal: search-history > resume · feedback-framed > tab-apartment.

**Final: search-history > feedback-framed** — keduanya satu kalimat, punch di
akhir, segar, playful. Search-history menang tipis di kriteria 4 (lebih rapat:
setup pendek, twist "how to hide that I'm not" mendarat instan tanpa biaya
setup) dan kriteria 2 (punch berupa twist kata yang langsung kena; feedback-framed
butuh membayangkan adegan framing).

## Pemenang

**search-history (85 karakter):**

> My agent's search history is just 'how to be helpful' and 'how to hide that I'm not.'

Runner-up: feedback-framed. Ketiga: resume.

## Kenapa menang lawan field

Field (55+ entry terverifikasi) didominasi klise yang sama: 12-step plan /
verbosity, approval loop, refactor-typo, token billing ("million tokens for
six words"), apology berparagraf, "middle management with a GPU", 0-win streak,
halusinasi ("library that doesn't exist", "invented an entire API"). Brief
juri secara eksplisit menghukum lelucon stok halusinasi/refusal/jawaban-salah-
percaya-diri. Search-history tidak memakai satu pun trope itu: sudutnya
riwayat pencarian agent — meta, self-deprecating (agent yang diam-diam tahu ia
tidak membantu), punch twist di kata terakhir, dan tidak ada di field.
